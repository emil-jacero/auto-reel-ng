## Why

The movie on the event page plays as one undivided file: the operator who wants the third chapter of a
forty-minute Midsommar movie drags the native timeline and guesses. D-15 left the list out on purpose, because the
render manifest recorded no chapter times and a browser exposes none of an MP4's chapters. `render-chapter-times`
now records each chapter's start in the rendered movie, and `movie-facts-read` puts those starts and a movie
version in the event detail. This change is the first user of them: it is small, read-only, and runs on the
detail alone, so it needs no proxy, no new route and no engine work.

## What Changes

- The "Movie" section lists the rendered movie's chapters under its player, from the event detail's chapter
  times: one row per chapter (number, name, start), each a button that moves the player to that start and plays
  (also before the first Play), reachable by Tab and Enter/Space, with no shortcut of the page's own.
- The row of the chapter the player is in is marked "Current chapter" in words with an icon (never colour alone,
  `aria-current`); it follows playback, pause and the native controls, without being announced.
- Chapter data the detail does not give (a movie rendered before `render-chapter-times`, an adopted legacy movie),
  or gives in a form that cannot be relied on, shows **no list** and no placeholder: a start time is never
  computed from clips, durations or the player.
- An outdated movie lists the chapters it has, labelled "As rendered".
- The section's facts also say the movie's version (the record's time and short fingerprint) when the detail
  gives it, and leave it out otherwise.
- No motion at all (a mark moves without a transition, a jump does not scroll the page), so reduced motion needs
  no special case; the list fits 320 to 1280 px and takes 44 px touch targets under a coarse pointer.
- Docs: HLD D-15's "no chapter list" sentence is amended, §4.10's v2 line notes the item delivered.
- Not changed: the movie route, the probe, the entity-tag `v`, the failure notes, Edit mode (still no movie), any
  `api/` or engine code, the `web` dependencies (no new runtime dependency, D-8).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `web-app`: MODIFIED "The event page plays the event's rendered movie" (a version in the facts, no longer "no
  chapter list", playback may also start from a chapter); ADDED "The movie player lists the rendered movie's
  chapters".

## Impact

- `web/` only: `src/movie/` gains `facts.ts`, `chapters.ts` (+ tests) and `ChapterList.tsx`; `MoviePanel.tsx`,
  `labels.ts` and `movie.css` change. `web/src/api/schema.d.ts` is read, not edited by hand (it is regenerated by
  `movie-facts-read`).
- Gates, merged first: `render-chapter-times` (the manifest's chapter starts) and `movie-facts-read` (the
  detail's `chapters` and version). If the detail does not give both, this change cannot be applied (task 1.1).
- Later users of the same pure functions: `timeline-view`'s chapter band (D-20).
