## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: The marked group turns together

**Reason**: The marks and their line are gone (`plain-clip-bin`); several clips are turned as a selection.

**Migration**: Select the clips with Ctrl/Cmd+click or Shift+click and press Rotate left or Rotate right in the Clip
tab, or choose them in the clip's menu ("The Clip tab acts on several selected clips", `web-app`). Each clip still
turns a quarter from its own turn, in one step.
